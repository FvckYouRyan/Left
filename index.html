Chapter I — Now

The world moves.
You stay still.

You open your phone and see another announcement. Someone got promoted. Someone got married. Someone moved to a new city with bright windows and a fresh start. And you close the app feeling more hollow than before you opened it.

This is the quiet grief that nobody talks about — not losing something you had, but watching others gain things you haven't found yet.

Chapter II — Then

You weren't always this quiet.

There was a version of you that believed things would unfold differently. You had plans, or at least you had the feeling that plans were coming. That something was about to click into place.

Click a memory below — not yours, but one that might feel familiar.

A few years ago

You had a plan.

It was vague, maybe. But it felt real. You imagined where you'd be by now — who you'd be with, what you'd have built. The plan didn't ask for a timeline. But the timeline came anyway.

Somewhere in between

You stopped counting birthdays.

Not because you got old, but because each one came with an invisible checklist you hadn't finished. So you started treating them as just another Tuesday, hoping no one would notice the weight you were carrying.

More recently

A friend moved on.

You were happy for them. You genuinely were. But there was a night — just one — where you sat alone and wondered if anyone would ever feel that way about staying close to you. Whether you were someone people move toward, or someone they drift away from.

Sometimes still

You revisit old conversations.

Messages you didn't send. Opportunities you let pass. Nights where you chose to stay in, and mornings where you wished you hadn't. You turn them over like stones, not sure what you're looking for.
